EduInvestor: Your Educational Funding Partner

EduInvestor is a digital platform for those seeking reliable and flexible educational funding options. At the heart of the platform is a wide and varied collection of investment opportunities tailored for education, each enriched with detailed descriptions, competitive rates of return, and comprehensive terms and conditions.

Courses Covered

Loan for Degree and PG Courses

Student loans for undergraduate studies can be availed by individuals who have completed secondary education and wish to undertake a three+ year course to enhance their job opportunities. These loans come with easy installment options.

Loan for PUC 1 and PUC 2

Student loans for PUC/1+2+ can be availed by students who have completed their 10th and wish to undertake a two-year pre-university college program. These loans comes with easy EMI options provide financial support to pursue Pre-university College Degree.

Loan for Certification Courses

Student loans for certification courses can be availed by students who wish to attend career-oriented certification programs, be it vocational, technical, or upskill courses being provided by the institutes. These are custom-made collateral-free loans.
